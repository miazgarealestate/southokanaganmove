Buying a Home in the South Okanagan

The steps, and the checks worth doing, for houses, townhomes, strata units and manufactured homes.

The basic steps

  1. Get your finances ready. Talk to a lender about pre-approval and your down payment.
  2. Define what you want. Community, property type, rural or in town, how you will use it.
  3. View properties. Take notes and ask about anything unclear.
  4. Make an offer with sensible conditions: financing, inspection, and strata or title review where relevant.
  5. Do your checks during the condition period, then remove subjects when satisfied.
  6. Complete the purchase through a lawyer or notary.

Inspections and checks

  • Home inspection by a qualified inspector.
  • Specialist checks where relevant: septic, well, roof, heating, electrical.
  • Title search and review of any covenants or right-of-way.
  • Permit history for additions, decks, finished basements and outbuildings.
  • Insurance quote before removing subjects.

Strata properties

Read the strata documents before you buy: bylaws, rules, minutes, financial statements, depreciation report, insurance and any special levies. Ask about rental restrictions and age limits. See the Province of BC strata housing page.

Manufactured homes

A manufactured home may be owned outright on its own land, or in a park where you own the home and pay pad rent. In a park, ask about the term of the tenancy, pad rent, park rules and the age and condition of the home. See Province of BC manufactured home park tenancies.

Acreage and rural homes

Read Buying rural property in BC. Wells, septic, access, zoning and wildfire insurance each need their own check.

Where to start
