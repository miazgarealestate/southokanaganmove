About Pat

Pat Miazga has been a REALTOR® in British Columbia since 2004. His real estate experience was built primarily in the Lower Mainland and includes residential and commercial property, along with construction, renovations and project management.

About three years ago he moved his family from the Lower Mainland to Anarchist Mountain, so he has been through the relocation process himself. He works with eXp Realty and offers practical, low-pressure advice.
