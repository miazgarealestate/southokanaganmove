What we collect

When you use the contact form we collect your name, email, optional phone number, your selected area of interest and relocation timeframe, and your message.

How it is used

Pat Miazga uses it to respond to your inquiry. Submissions are delivered through Formspree, a third-party form service.

Consent and withdrawal

By ticking the consent box you agree to be contacted about your inquiry. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting Pat at 604-562-5982.
