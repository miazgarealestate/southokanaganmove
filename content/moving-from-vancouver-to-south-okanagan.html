Pat has made this move

Pat Miazga moved his own family from the Lower Mainland to Anarchist Mountain about three years ago, after a real estate career built mainly in the Lower Mainland. This guide reflects both the professional and the personal side of the process.

Sell first or buy first?

Most people moving from Vancouver are selling a home and buying another. There is no single right order. Selling first removes risk but can leave you looking for a place under time pressure. Buying first gives you choice but can mean carrying two properties. Talk it through with your lender, your accountant and Pat before you list.

Plan the money with professionals

  • Talk to your lender about whether you can port or must replace your mortgage, and about the cost of breaking a term.
  • Ask an accountant how the sale will be taxed. A principal residence is generally treated differently from other property, but individual circumstances vary.
  • Budget for Property Transfer Tax, legal fees, moving costs and selling costs. Check the current Province of BC page for rates and exemptions.

What changes in daily life

  • Driving distances are longer and transit is limited.
  • Services are in smaller centres. Confirm healthcare and family-doctor access early.
  • Rural homes often have a well, septic and private road. See Buying rural property.
  • Smoke and heat in summer, and different winter conditions from the Lower Mainland.
  • Your income and work: confirm whether you can work remotely and what internet is actually available at the address.

Buying from a distance

  • Make at least one trip in each of two seasons if you can.
  • Ask for video walkthroughs, but do not replace an in-person look and an inspection.
  • Schedule the inspection and any septic, well or specialist checks while you are in town.
  • Line up a lawyer or notary and your insurance before you remove subjects.

A sensible timeline

  1. Visit and shortlist communities. See Osoyoos vs. Oliver.
  2. Meet your lender and accountant.
  3. Decide on sell-first or buy-first.
  4. Prepare and list your current home, if applicable.
  5. Search, view and inspect properties.
  6. Close, move and settle in.
