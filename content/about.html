Pat Miazga has been a licensed REALTOR® in British Columbia since 2004. His experience was built primarily in the Lower Mainland and covers residential and commercial real estate, along with construction, renovations and project management.

About three years ago, Pat moved his family to Anarchist Mountain above Osoyoos. That means he understands relocating from the Lower Mainland from the inside, including what surprises people.

How Pat works

  • Practical, plain-spoken advice with no pressure.
  • A close look at how a property is built, serviced and maintained, drawing on his construction and renovation background.
  • Help for people relocating from out of town, including buying at a distance.

Brokerage

Pat Miazga, REALTOR® works with eXp Realty (eXp Realty of Canada, Inc.).
