Sources checked 12 November 2026. General guidance, not engineering, health or legal advice. Costs and results vary by property, so get quotes and reports from qualified professionals.

Wells

  • Look up the record. Wells are searchable in the province's GWELLS database. Since 29 February 2016, well drillers and pump installers must submit a report within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well. See BC: well records and registration.
  • Older wells may have no record. Domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, and owners are strongly encouraged to register. If no record exists for a well built before 2016, the owner can file a domestic well registration form. See domestic well registration.
  • Ask for the construction record, the pump and pressure-tank details, and any past test results.
  • Test it yourself. Ask a qualified well or pump professional for a flow (yield) test, and a lab for a water-quality test that includes bacteria. Choose the tests with their advice, and ask for the results in writing.
  • Non-domestic use. If you plan irrigation or other non-domestic use, ask the Province whether a licence is needed. See buying rural property in BC.

Septic

  • Under the Sewerage System Regulation, an owner who wants to build, alter or repair a sewerage system must use an Authorized Person: a Registered Onsite Wastewater Practitioner (ROWP) or a professional (such as an engineer). See Fraser Health owner Q&A (the rule is provincial; the local health authority handles filings).
  • Ask the seller for the filing record, the Letter of Certification, the plans and the maintenance and pumping records. Work on a system is expected to follow the province's Standard Practice Manual.
  • A general home inspection does not normally assess a septic system in depth. Hire a qualified septic inspector, and treat the findings as part of the price discussion.
  • If you plan an addition, a second dwelling or more bedrooms, ask the authorized person whether the existing system can serve it before you commit.
