Living in Oliver

An agricultural and wine-growing town with the region's main hospital, set among orchards and vineyards.

The short version

Oliver is a small town about 27 km south of Penticton on Highway 97. It is surrounded by orchards and vineyards and is less resort-oriented than Osoyoos. The South Okanagan General Hospital is in Oliver, which matters to many people planning to retire here.

Housing you will see

  • Single-family homes in town and in nearby subdivisions.
  • Townhomes and condominiums, plus manufactured homes and parks.
  • Small farms, orchards and vineyard properties, many of which are in the Agricultural Land Reserve.
  • Rural properties outside town limits, which fall under RDOS Electoral Area C rather than the Town.

If you are buying farmland or a vineyard

This is a different purchase from a home. The Agricultural Land Commission sets rules for land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, including what you can build and how you can use the land. Water for irrigation, the condition of orchards or vines, and whether a farm-class property tax status applies are all separate questions. Get professional advice on each. See Buying rural property in BC.

Services

Oliver has its own Town government, and the town's recent recruitment efforts show that physician availability is a live issue. Ask about family-doctor access before you rely on it. For schools, see School District 53.

Things to check before you buy in Oliver

  • Town or RDOS jurisdiction, and the zoning that applies.
  • ALR status, if any, via the Agricultural Land Commission.
  • Water source: municipal, irrigation district or well. Confirm what is actually included in the sale.
  • Wildfire and insurance exposure for properties on the edges of town.

Property guides: manufactured homes and wells and septic.
