Why people make the move

The South Okanagan isn't
a compromise. It's an upgrade.

Every week, buyers from Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Saskatoon, and beyond ask the same questions. Here's what actually drives the decision.

01

Your equity goes four times further

A $1.2M Vancouver condo gets you a 2,400 sq ft home on a half-acre in Osoyoos — with money left over. Prairie buyers find prices surprisingly competitive with Calgary and Saskatoon, with a climate upgrade that doesn't cost extra.

02

The climate is genuinely different

Canada's only designated desert sits in this valley. 2,039 hours of sunshine per year. Summers that run May through October. Winters that are a fraction of what Saskatchewan, Alberta, or even Vancouver delivers. People visit once and never stop thinking about it.

03

The tax picture is cleaner here

Osoyoos and Oliver are outside the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax designated zone — you simply don't declare. Compare that to Kelowna or Penticton where SVT applies. Combined with lower purchase prices, the total cost of ownership looks very different.

04

It's a real community, not a resort town

Schools, healthcare, grocery, recreation — it's all here. The kind of place where people show up for each other. That matters when you're making a full relocation, not just a vacation property play.

"Winter here means skiing at Mt Baldy — 55 minutes from Osoyoos, 35 minutes from Anarchist Mountain. Then back to the lake valley before dark."

— Mt Baldy Ski Area, above Rock Creek BC
